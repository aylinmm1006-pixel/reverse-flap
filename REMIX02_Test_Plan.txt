REMIX 02 — Five checks to perform in your browser

   Expected: overlay disappears; bird and pipe animate; score begins at 0.
2. CONTROLS: Hold Up/W and Down/S, and try the on-screen buttons.
   Expected: pipe opening moves vertically, staying within the canvas.
3. SPECIAL RULE: Keep the pipe opening aligned with the bird as it approaches.
   Expected: bird moves by itself; only the pipe gap responds to controls; each safe pass earns 1 point.
4. ENDING: Intentionally collide with a pipe, then play again and try to pass five pipes.
   Expected: collision displays Game over; five safe passes display You win; movement stops at either ending.
5. RESTART: After an ending, click Play again. Also try the Restart button mid-round.
   Expected: fresh score of 0 and a new round; Restart returns to the opening screen.
